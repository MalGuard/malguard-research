RESEARCH PRACTICE

Methodology & disclosure

We distinguish direct observations, external reports and interpretations, and state the limits of each.

Publication disclosure: MalGuard Research currently publishes original technical notes. They are not independent peer-reviewed journal articles, certifications, or validated product detection benchmarks.

Research scope

Our current publications examine the security implications of game-mod distribution, archive structure, file identity, defensive scanning, and malware-related trust boundaries. They are conceptual analyses grounded in public security guidance. No real-world infection rates, detector accuracy measurements, or laboratory test results are claimed.

Evidence taxonomy

We separate directly observed properties (for example a file digest or archive inventory), externally reported context (for example provenance or dated reputation information), and analyst interpretation. Each layer has different limitations. An absence of observed indicators must not be translated into a universal assurance of safety.

Threat models and examples

Hypothetical examples are labeled as illustrative, not empirical case studies. A threat model names the protected assets, trust boundaries, possible adverse outcomes, assumptions, and controls. The goal is to reason clearly about defensive decisions without publishing instructions for creating or deploying harmful software.

Source quality and citations

Technical background is linked to relevant primary references where possible, including NIST, MITRE ATT&CK, OWASP, and SLSA documentation. A reference supports a background concept; it does not imply that the referenced organization reviewed, endorsed, or validated MalGuard's work. Claims specific to MalGuard are identified as proposals or project observations unless independently measured.

Reproducibility and PDF editions

Each paper has an HTML edition and a PDF edition generated automatically from the published article during the GitHub Pages deployment. The GTA VI advisory is formatted into a fixed 15-page A4 PDF edition. A locally bundled page-turn engine presents a hard-cover book edition with content flowed into numbered print-style pages. These are alternate reading formats, not separate experimental evidence. The book edition is a reading adaptation; the HTML paper remains the canonical citation target. The publication source and PDF generation script are available in the public repository.

Editorial checks before deployment

The deployment workflow now verifies that each publication has a canonical URL, a disclosure of peer-review status, multiple linked external references, and a generated PDF. It also checks local links and confirms the book-rendering library was packaged. These automated checks catch publishing mistakes; they do not independently validate scientific claims or guarantee performance in every browser.

Limitations and next steps

These notes do not constitute a systematic literature review or experimental validation. Future empirical work would need clearly defined sampling, consent, ground-truth criteria, test environments, documented methods, versioned datasets, error rates, and independent review. Until then, no claims of proven superiority, accuracy or comprehensive malware protection should be inferred.

Corrections and transparency

Corrections, suggestions, and reproducibility issues can be submitted through the public issue tracker. All seven publications and the site source are available in the GitHub repository. We will distinguish editorial revisions from new evidence or experimental results.
